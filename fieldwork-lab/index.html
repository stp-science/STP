Your counting rule

  1. Count each organism by the centre of its symbol.
  2. Include centres on the bottom and left edges.
  3. Exclude centres on the top and right edges.
  4. Count each species separately.

A model habitat for practising ecological sampling. Distances are model units. No login or student data entry is required within the activity.

Small samples, careful science.

  1. Choose an activity and read the target location.
  2. Move the frame until its bottom-left corner matches that coordinate.
  3. Check the position, then count the organisms yourself.
  4. Write each count in the matching row of your paper table.
  5. Mark it recorded and move to the next sample.

The frame always covers 5 × 5 model units. In transects it moves along a line at fixed intervals.
